## Section 2: Job Creation Vision
CivicEye AI is designed not to replace human labor, but to modernize public administration, protect field crews, and generate legitimate technology-enabled careers:

1. **Civic Technology Support Specialists:** First-line technical agents maintaining portal uptime and municipal citizen helpdesks.
2. **Municipal GIS & Spatial Operators:** Cartographers and GIS technicians reviewing spatial clusters, heatmaps, and ward boundaries.
3. **AI Vision & Data Technicians:** Annotation specialists and ML engineers verifying ground-truth detection accuracy on local road and weather topologies.
4. **IoT & Edge Gateway Technicians:** Field specialists installing and calibrating authorized telemetry devices and municipal environmental sensors.
5. **Certified Civic Drone Operators:** Licensed commercial drone pilots executing scheduled aerial infrastructure surveys.
6. **Cybersecurity & Compliance Officers:** Security analysts monitoring access control, audit logs, and citizen privacy compliance.
7. **Civic Operations Coordinators:** Ward dispatch managers coordinating inter-departmental infrastructure campaigns.
